Before you go

Shop on the mainland

Do the big shop in Cleveland before the ferry. The island shops have less range and cost more. Grab the 2 hot chickens last.

Freeze the burger mince

Burgers are Monday night, 2½ days after the shop. Freeze the 1 kg of burger mince flat in a zip-lock bag. It thaws slowly in the esky and keeps everything else cold.

Prep at home

Dice the ragu veg into a zip-lock bag, and make the chimichurri the night before and bring it in a jar. It tastes better after a day.

Esky order

Eat in order of freshness: burrata Saturday, chicken Sunday lunch, steak Sunday night, roast beef and burgers Monday. Top up ice on the island.
